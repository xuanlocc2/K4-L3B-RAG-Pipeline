## A/B comparison

- **Better configuration on this dataset:** Config A (dense-only) —
  outperforms Config B on context precision, recall, and source hit rate
  by 2–5 points absolute. The difference is driven mostly by case Q19
  (see "Worst performers").
- **Why hybrid underperformed on this corpus:** RRF fuses *per chunk*,
  while golden sources are *per document*. BM25 tends to surface several
  chunks from the same popular news article (article_01 and article_02),
  pushing rare-but-correct chunks (article_08) out of the top-5 even
  though dense alone ranked them correctly. With `top_k=5` and a small
  corpus (276 chunks), the diversification effect of BM25 hurts more than
  it helps.
- **Trade-off on latency:** hybrid is **~30% faster** end-to-end because
  BM25 returns instantly without embedding the query twice; dense-only
  pays one extra encode for the fallback check inside `retrieve()`.
- **Failure safety:** both pipelines correctly produce safe-refusal
  answers for the two out-of-domain cases (Q20, Q21).
